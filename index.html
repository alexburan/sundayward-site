Sundayward walks with you from one Sunday to the next. The Bible, the seasons of the church year, notes on the sermon you heard, the people you pray for, and your own church, together in one calm app.

Coming to iPhone in early 2027. For Protestant, Catholic and Orthodox Christians, in English and Spanish.

This is the day that the LORD has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

Psalm 118:24 · Berean Standard Bible

What is inside

The whole BibleBerean Standard Bible in English and Reina-Valera in Spanish, on the phone, free, and readable with no signal.
The church yearThe app's colour follows Advent, Christmas, Lent and Easter, on the Western or the Orthodox calendar.
Sermon notesLive captions while your pastor preaches, then a short summary to carry into the week. Listening happens on the phone.
Prayer listKeep the names you are praying for, get a gentle reminder, and note the prayers that were answered.
Your churchFind it on the map or add it by hand: service times, directions, and a way to invite a friend.

Private by design

  • No account and no sign-in. Nothing to create, nothing to forget.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.
  • Your prayers, notes and church stay on your iPhone.
  • Scripture is always free.
