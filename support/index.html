Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sundayward has no sign-up and no sign-in. Everything you add is kept on your iPhone.

I got a new iPhone. How do I keep my prayers and notes?

Your Sundayward data travels with your iPhone backup. Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, and Sundayward comes back as you left it.

Is the Bible free?

Yes, always. Scripture is never behind a subscription. The Bible text is the Berean Standard Bible in English and Reina-Valera in Spanish, and it works with no internet connection.

Why is the app a different colour today?

Sundayward follows the church year. The colour changes with the seasons: Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter, Pentecost and Ordinary Time. Orthodox Christians can choose the Orthodox calendar in Settings, and the dates follow it.

Does the app record my church service?

Only when you start it. Live captions use your iPhone's own speech recognition, and the audio never leaves your phone. Please follow your church's guidance on recording during services.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sundayward. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot see or refund purchases ourselves.

I bought Plus but the app does not show it.

Open Settings in Sundayward and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

My church is not on the map.

Tap Add my church and type its name, address and service times. It stays on your phone.
